Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. As you use the service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core Spendly functionality: displaying your expense history, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted service providers for functions like hosting and font delivery, who process data only as necessary to provide those services.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account settings or the contact details provided on our website.
